What we'll do

Sunlight is pouring down on us all the time — how much can it actually do? In this session, kids and their grown-ups experiment with small solar panels, motors, and batteries to find out.

Working as a team, each family builds a little solar-powered machine — a spinning wheel, a buzzing motor, a wobbly "bad tractor" — and gets to take it home.

We'll explore

  • How a solar panel turns light into electricity
  • What "voltage" and "current" mean (with hands-on meters you can watch move)
  • Why a cloudy day is trickier than a sunny one
  • How motors, wheels, and gears turn electricity into motion

You'll leave with

  • A small solar machine your family built together
  • A feel for how off-grid power really works
  • A pile of good questions about energy

Who it's for

Curious kids ages 6–12 and one grown-up per child. No experience needed — just bring curiosity and clothes you can get a little dirty in.

This session is an outdoor, weather-friendly version of the Solar 101 workshops we run at Waterbear Field School.

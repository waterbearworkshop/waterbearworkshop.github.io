What we'll do

Fire is one of the oldest tools there is — and there's real science to making it burn clean, hot, and safe. In this session, families build a small fire in a rocket stove (a clever design that makes a little wood go a long way) and cook up a snack together.

Along the way, kids learn how air, fuel, and heat work together, and how to be calm and careful around fire.

We'll explore

  • What fire needs to burn — and burn cleanly
  • Why a rocket stove is so efficient
  • Fire safety, hands-on and unhurried
  • Cooking a simple snack over the flames

You'll leave with

  • A full belly and a new respect for fire
  • Real fire-safety skills
  • An understanding of off-grid cooking

Who it's for

Kids ages 7–12 and one grown-up per child. Grown-ups stay hands-on for this one — we work with fire as a team.
